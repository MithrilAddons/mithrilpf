The Curator leaderboard now stays up to date while you play.

**Changes**
- The leaderboard refreshes when you open `/games` or switch to it, after your own guess, and every minute while it's on screen. Previously it could keep showing standings from earlier in the session, missing players who finished after you.

**Updating**
Open `/mpf → Updates`, enable **Include pre-releases**, then select **Check now**. Once the update is ready, quit Minecraft normally and wait a few seconds before relaunching.
