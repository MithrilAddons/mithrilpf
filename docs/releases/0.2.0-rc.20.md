Commands now live under `/mpf`, and the finder's Records tab is a PB viewer for your splits and rooms.

**Commands**
- `/mpf help` lists every command. `/mpf pbs [floor]` opens your PBs, `/mpf status` shows tracking status, and `/mpf invite`, `/mpf chat` and `/mpf games` replace the older commands.
- `/mpc` and `/games` stay, and the old command names keep working for now.
- Everything MithrilPF says in chat now starts with its icon and name, so it stands out from other messages.

**PB viewer**
- Records has a floor dropdown that starts on the floor you last played. Floors without PBs are greyed out.
- **Overview** shows the stats parties check beside your local solo and terminals PBs.
- **Splits** lists every split PB for the floor, real time with tick time.
- **Rooms** lists your room PBs: midclear and regular clears, secrets per minute and Total, with a search box. Hover a time to see its tick time.

**Updating**
Open `/mpf → Updates`, enable **Include pre-releases**, then select **Check now**. Once the update is ready, quit Minecraft normally and wait a few seconds before relaunching.
