Room PBs now work in party runs and tell midclears from regular clears, and the party finder and Games screens are easier to use.

**Room PBs**
- Room PBs are tracked in every run, not just soloclears. Each room counts on its own: the clear counts if no teammate was in the room before it was cleared, and the secrets count if nobody else was there when they were found.
- A room is a **midclear** when you found at least half its secrets (up to three) during the clear. Midclear and regular rooms keep separate clear and secrets PBs, secrets are shown per minute, and a new **Total** covers rooms finished with every secret.
- Results show when you leave a cleared room. Coming back for more secrets updates them.
- Secrets and Total only save once the run reaches 300 score (a soloclear's 300, or the estimate at boss entry, ignoring deaths). Until then they show **needs 300**.
- Your existing secrets PBs become Total PBs.

**Party finder**
- The mouse wheel scrolls the party list, party details and chat.
- Lists keep their place when you click or when parties refresh.
- The finder HUD moves below boss bars and hides while the player list is open.
- Tidier editor and labels, and long text ends in "…" instead of being cut off.

**Games and HUD**
- Curator's result card and leaderboard fit small windows, and your own leaderboard row always shows.
- The timer HUD shrinks to fit the screen instead of running off it.

**Updating**
Open `/mpf → Updates`, enable **Include pre-releases**, then select **Check now**. Once the update is ready, quit Minecraft normally and wait a few seconds before relaunching.
