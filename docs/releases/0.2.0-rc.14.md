Party invites now start only when you click: the mod no longer sends Hypixel party commands on its own.

**Changes**
- As party leader, click anywhere in chat, use `/mpfinvite`, or press the finder screen's button to invite. Each click sends a single `/p` for the players still missing, at most once every ten seconds.
- `/party list` is no longer sent. The mod follows your Hypixel party from chat while you play and, after a restart, asks Hypixel's Mod API instead.
- A game party led by someone else, or with players outside the finder party, blocks invites until it changes. The listing closes once all five players have joined.

**Compatibility**
- Requires [Hypixel Mod API](https://modrinth.com/mod/hypixel-mod-api) 1.0.2 or newer. The updater never installs other mods: add it to your `mods` folder and restart first. Until then, Updates reports that newer releases need different dependencies.

**Updating**
Open `/mpf → Updates`, enable **Include pre-releases**, then select **Check now**. Once the update is ready, quit Minecraft normally and wait a few seconds before relaunching.
