MithrilPF now has a Games screen, starting with Curator: a daily SkyBlock item guessing game.

**Changes**
- `/games` (or `/mpfgames`) opens the Games screen. You can also bind a key to it in Controls.
- **Curator:** guess the day's SkyBlock item in up to ten tries. Each guess shows how its rarity, type, museum category, game stage, requirements, soulbound status, origin, market value, NPC price and name length compare with the answer, with arrows pointing towards it. Hover over a cell for details.
- Everyone gets the same item, and a new one arrives at 00:00 UTC. Finished rounds reveal the item and let you copy your result to share.
- The **Leaderboard** view ranks the month's season by points, with your own rank, streak and guess history.

**Updating**
Open `/mpf → Updates`, enable **Include pre-releases**, then select **Check now**. Once the update is ready, quit Minecraft normally and wait a few seconds before relaunching.

On 0.2.0-rc.15 with pre-releases enabled, the update window on the title screen also offers this version; choose **Install on exit**.
