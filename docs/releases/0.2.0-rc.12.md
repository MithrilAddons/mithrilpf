New solo-clear replays show room secrets increasing as their counters are recorded.

**Changes**
- Records room-secret counter changes through 300 score, including repeat visits and multi-tile rooms.
- The linked map updates room counts during playback and when scrubbing backward or forward.
- Older replays keep their final room counts because they have no room-counter history.

**Updating**
Open `/mpf → Updates`, enable **Include pre-releases**, then select **Check now**. Once the update is ready, quit Minecraft normally and wait a few seconds before relaunching.

Start a fresh run after updating to record room-secret changes. Counter updates can arrive after the actual pickup.
